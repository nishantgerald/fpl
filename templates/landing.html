How these projections are built

  • Expected minutes first. A hot-form substitute is not a better pick than a nailed starter, however good his per-90 numbers look.
  • Each fixture scored on its own, so a blank gameweek scores zero and a double scores twice — rather than an average that hides both.
  • Every component priced separately and summed, so the breakdown is the projection rather than a story printed beside it.
  • Rates from small samples are regressed toward the positional average. Two minutes of football is not evidence of anything.
